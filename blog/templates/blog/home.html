Naagin: Keeper of the Hidden Spring

At the edge of a sleeping village, an ancient spring shimmered beneath the banyan roots. The elders spoke of its guardian—a Naagin, a shape-shifting serpent spirit who protected the water and the life it sustained. One moonlit night, a young traveller heard the spring calling. What they discovered was a reminder that courage often begins with listening.
